### [Android TV for PS4](https://github.com/odiecay/android-tv-ps4)

Boots Android TV on PS4 hardware from a homebrew application, with access to Android apps, streaming services, and the Play Store.

---

### [Kali Linux for PS4](https://github.com/blue0x1/kali-ps4)

Kali Linux distribution with XFCE desktop for jailbroken PS4 consoles, featuring an on-console graphical installer and support for internal or USB-based installations.
